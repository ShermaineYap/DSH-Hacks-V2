Fasa

Dengue at home, one day at a time. Rest at home, clinic today, or hospital now, following the Malaysian MOH dengue guideline.

Shermaine Yap · DSH Hacks V2 (AI × Healthcare) · Live: dsh-hacks-v2-jade.vercel.app · Code: github.com/ShermaineYap/DSH-Hacks-V2

The problem

Malaysia has recorded 79,686 dengue cases in 2026 (to 31 Oct). Most patients are sent home. The MOH CPG says the critical phase usually starts after day 3, often as the fever falls; outpatients should be reviewed daily from day 3; and any of 8 warning signs means hospital. Patients go home with a paper monitoring record that has no space for symptoms. The one Malaysian app pilot (DengueAid, n=37) showed limited utility.

What Fasa does

  • Day clock from the first day of fever, with the critical days 3–7 shaded and the next blood test always shown.
  • Check-in by tapping or writing in Malay, English or both. Gemini and on-device rules find the CPG warning signs; union merge, so the model can add a sign but never remove one.
  • Slip reading with Gemini vision. Every value is shown beside its printed text and confirmed by the person, and big jumps are flagged. Tesseract is the offline fallback.
  • Rules engine: deterministic code transcribed from CPG Tables 2–4, 7.3.1 and Appendix 9, including the Malaysian HCT lines. Every reason cites its table.
  • Trend charts and a printable handover in the MOH record layout, plus warning signs.
  • Bahasa Melayu / English, a demo patient, no account; the record stays on the phone.

How it is built

Next.js 15 + TypeScript on Vercel. rules.ts decides; lexicon.ts is the offline Malay/English extractor; /api/extract and /api/read-slip call Gemini with JSON schemas at temperature 0 and discover a working Flash model at runtime. Only a message or photo the person chooses goes to Google’s Gemini API; Fasa’s server stores nothing.

Text set: 61 held-out messages written by a separate agent; rules tuned only on the other half. Slips: 30 mock FBC slips from a different random seed than the tuning set.

Limits and next steps

Not tested with patients or clinicians; synthetic test data; adult CPG only; some signs (tender liver, fluid accumulation) cannot be checked at home, so Fasa keeps people going to daily clinic reviews rather than replacing them. Not a medical device. Next: review by Malaysian primary-care doctors, a small usability study, WhatsApp check-ins, and Tamil/Chinese support.
